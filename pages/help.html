Frequently Asked Questions

What is CineVerse?

CineVerse is your place to discover movies and series. Browse by genre, see what's trending, watch trailers, read what each title is about and save favourites to My List.

How do I find a movie or series?

Use the search box at the top of any page (press / or Ctrl K, or ⌘ K on a Mac). You can search by title, actor, director, genre or year. You can also open Movies or Series and filter by genre or sort by rating.

Can I watch full movies on CineVerse?

Yes. Titles marked Free play in full right here: open one and select Watch Now. If you stop part-way, the title appears under Continue Watching on the homepage so you can pick up where you left off. For other movies, open the title and select Where to Watch to see which streaming services have it.

How do I watch a trailer?

Open any title and select Play Trailer. Use the controls under the picture to pause, skip 10 seconds, change the volume or speed, or go full screen. Most trailers come from the studios' YouTube channels; if a studio doesn't allow its trailer on other websites, the player shows a Watch on YouTube button instead.

How do I change the video quality or turn on subtitles?

While a video plays, select the gear button at the bottom right and choose Auto or a quality such as 720p. Auto picks the best quality for your screen and connection. Select the subtitles button next to it to choose a language. CineVerse remembers your choices for next time. Trailers from YouTube use YouTube's own quality and caption settings.

What are the player shortcuts?

Space or K play or pause, ← and → skip back or forward 10 seconds, ↑ and ↓ change the volume, M mutes, F switches to full screen, C turns subtitles on or off and Esc closes the player. On a phone, tap to show the controls and double-tap the left or right side to skip 10 seconds.

How does My List work?

Select the ＋ button on any poster, or My List on a title's details. Saved titles appear on your My List page. Your list is stored in this browser, so it stays after you close the tab.

Why don't I see my list on another device?

My List, your profile name and viewing history are stored on the device you're using. Using another browser or device, or clearing your browsing data, starts a fresh list.

How do I change my profile name?

Select your avatar in the top-right corner, then choose Add your name or Edit profile. Your avatar shows the first letter of your name.

Can I share a title with friends?

Yes. Open any title and select the share icon. On phones you'll see your device's share menu; on computers the link is copied so you can paste it anywhere.

How do I clear my data?

Select your avatar and choose Clear my data. This removes My List, recently viewed titles and your profile name from this browser.

How do I unsubscribe from the newsletter?

Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also contact us and we'll remove your address.
